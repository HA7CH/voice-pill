Voice Pill for macOS

1. Open the DMG and drag Voice Pill onto Applications.
2. Eject the disk and open Voice Pill from Applications.
3. Version 0.2.5 is Developer ID-signed and Apple-notarized. macOS may show the normal first-download confirmation.
4. Allow microphone and Accessibility, then reopen Voice Pill if needed.
5. Choose Doubao for account-free dictation, or use your signed-in Codex account.
6. Set the Globe/Fn key action to Do Nothing in System Settings > Keyboard.

Do not disable Gatekeeper. Download only from HA7CH/voice-pill releases.
